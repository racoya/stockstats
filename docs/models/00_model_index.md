# Mathematical Models Index: The Logic Engine

## Overview
This directory contains the deep-dive theoretical blueprints, mathematical equations, and Python implementation architecture for the core quantitative algorithms utilized by the STOCKSTATS platform. 

The models are segmented into four distinct stages of the trade lifecycle: **Signal Generation**, **Signal Optimization**, **Sizing & Execution**, and **Validation**.

## Phase 1: Signal Generation (The Edge)
These models are responsible for discovering pure structural and temporal anomalies in the market data.
*   **[01. Dynamic Volatility (GARCH)](01_garch_volatility.md):** Replaces static Standard Deviation. Uses Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity to instantly expand trading bands during market shocks, preventing false mean-reversion entries.
*   **[02. Cointegration & StatArb](02_cointegration_arb.md):** Replaces Pearson correlation. Utilizes Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) tests to mathematically prove the absolute spread between two assets is stationary (reliably mean-reverting) before authorizing pairs trading.
*   **[03. Regime Detection (HMM)](03_hmm_regime_detection.md):** Uses Gaussian Hidden Markov Models and transition probability matrices to autonomously flag if the macro market is "Choppy" (authorize mean-reversion) or "Trending" (hard veto mean-reversion).

## Phase 2: Signal Optimization (The Modifiers)
Once a signal is generated, these models dynamically optimize its entry parameters to eliminate lag and predict its real-world viability.
*   **[07. Kalman Filters](07_kalman_filters.md):** Replaces static, backward-looking OLS moving averages. A recursive algorithm that calculates the true "hidden state" of the Hedge Ratio ($\beta$) tick-by-tick, eliminating lag in spreads and pairs trades.
*   **[08. Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (Half-Life)](08_ornstein_uhlenbeck_halflife.md):** Calculates the exact expected duration (Half-Life) of a mean-reverting spread via AR(1) regression. Acts as a veto if a statistically sound trade will take too long to revert (capital lockup risk).
*   **[09. Fractional Differencing](09_fractional_differencing.md):** Applies fractional calculus to non-stationary price data ($d \approx 0.45$). Achieves mandatory stationarity for Machine Learning while perfectly preserving the long-term memory that standard integer differencing ($d=1$) destroys.

## Phase 3: Sizing & Execution (Capital Deployment)
These models dictate exactly how capital is deployed into the validated signal, mathematically isolating absolute edge and eliminating capital ruin.
*   **[04. VWAP & Liquidity Profiling](04_vwap_liquidity.md):** Creates historical volume profiles. Slices large parent orders into micro-orders via Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP) to prevent sweeping the order book and incurring negative slippage.
*   **[05. Expectancy & System Quality (SQN)](05_expectancy_and_sqn.md):** Normalizes all trades to an Initial Risk multiplier ($1R$). Calculates the true mathematical Expected Value ($E(R)$) and optimizes stop-losses dynamically via historical Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE).
*   **[06. Copulas & Fractional Kelly](06_copula_kelly_sizing.md):** Uses Copula functions to calculate "Fat Tail" crash dependency (systemic risk) between active assets. Sizes positions geometrically based on the algorithm's win rate using a constrained Fractional Kelly Criterion ($0.25f^*$).

## Phase 4: Validation (The Overlays)
These models serve as the final confirmation overlays and stress-testers to ensure the structural integrity of the entire trading desk.
*   **[10. Machine Learning Overlays (Meta-Labeling)](10_machine_learning_overlays.md):** Employs non-linear classifiers (XGBoost/Random Forests) *not* to discover trades, but strictly to predict the probability of success for trades already generated by Phase 1 models, dynamically altering position sizing.
*   **[11. Backtesting Rigor & Bias Elimination](11_backtesting_rigor_and_bias.md):** Mandates Point-in-Time data to eliminate survivorship/look-ahead bias, and utilizes the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) to mathematically penalize the system for data mining and overfitting parameter searches.
*   **[12. Parametric Value at Risk (VaR)](12_value_at_risk_var.md):** The Master Kill Switch. Calculates the maximum potential dollar loss of the entire aggregated portfolio over a 24-hour period (99% confidence) using the dynamic GARCH covariance matrix.

## Phase 5: Real-World Fail-Safes (Microstructure Defenses)
These protocols protect the mathematically perfect logic engines from the chaotic reality of broken exchange APIs and predatory high-frequency market makers.
*   **[13. Order State Machine & Reconciliation](13_state_machine_reconciliation.md):** Utilizes deterministic UUIDs (`clientOid`) to force the Execution Engine to safely halt, query, and resolve orphaned "Zombie" trades during Exchange API timeouts/glitches without duplicating risk.
*   **[14. Data Scrubbing & Hampel Filters](14_data_scrubbing_hampel.md):** The sterilization gate. Uses rolling Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) to algorithmically scrub extreme data errors ("rogue ticks") broadcast by exchanges before they can poison the downstream GARCH or OLS arrays.
*   **[15. Order Book Imbalance (OBI) & Toxicity](15_orderbook_toxicity_obi.md):** Detects Adverse Selection and L2 "Spoofing" by predatory HFTs. Calculates the ratio of limit bid/ask pressure to dynamically pause the VWAP Execution Slicer until the toxic flow subsides.
